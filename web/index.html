vitohome catalog finder

Paste the line the hub logs at boot and get the datapoint catalog that matches your Viessmann controller.

Your device

All values are hexadecimal, exactly as logged. The line appears as Device identification: … at start-up and as Device: … in the configuration dump. The GFA chip selects the burner-control fault texts; the hub logs it in the same line as GFA=0x24 (read from 0x7650 on P300/KW units that have a burner control). F0 is logged as F0=0x1E00, the two raw bytes at 0x00F0, which Vitosoft reads as one little-endian number (1E00 is F0 30); it only matters where several variants share a software index. Up to three digits are taken as F0 in decimal, as Vitosoft writes it (30 for VScotHO1_200_30).

Catalog language

Entity names, option labels and fault texts come from Vitosoft in that language; units are the same in both. Home Assistant derives entity IDs from the names, so switching the language of an installed catalog creates new entities. Other languages: scripts/gen_catalog.py --culture fr (or it, nl, pl, …) from your own Vitosoft export.

Lookup data: resolve.json, generated by scripts/gen_catalog.py --export-all from the Vitosoft export. Project: esphome_vitohome.
